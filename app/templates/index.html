Risk decisions you can inspect.

Three models from our 23CSE301 Machine Learning capstone. Each one answers a question a risk team asks every day. Enter a case or load a real record from the data, and the model gives its answer and shows which inputs moved it.

How each model was built

  1. Audit

    Shape, types, missing values, duplicates and impossible values checked in each dataset.

  2. Clean and engineer

    Justified imputation, outlier treatment, and new ratio features such as loan-to-income and credit utilisation.

  3. Compare

    10 regression, 10 classification and 2 clustering algorithms, each family on one identical split.

  4. Tune and validate

    GridSearchCV and 5-fold cross-validation; elbow, silhouette and dendrogram to choose k.

  5. Deploy

    The chosen models are saved with joblib and served by this Flask app, reproducing the notebook predictions.
